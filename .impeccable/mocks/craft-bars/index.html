Linear + Stripe + GitHub

Dense and fast. 36px rows, inline summary strip, underline tabs, accent only on primary and focus.

Premium and considered. 44px rows, four-figure summary bar, segmented tabs, soft-tint status.

Best number alignment and the most polished on a projector; calm rather than fast.

03

Workspace Admin + Eventbrite

The literal category. 48px rows, three KPI cards, filled status pills, blue links.

Maximum familiarity, lowest distinctiveness — and the closest to the old look's DNA.

04

Notion + Linear + Vercel

Balanced. 40px rows, quiet count line, no card chrome on filters, monochrome links, graphite primary.

Most air on detail pages, still tight on lists. Hardest to hold together across 35 screens.
